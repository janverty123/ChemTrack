# ChemTrack webfonts

Self-hosted variable WOFF2 fonts, each with its SIL Open Font License file.

- **Bricolage Grotesque** (`bricolage-grotesque-variable.woff2`, Latin subset, weights 200–800): headings, numbers and the wordmark. From the Fontsource distribution of the Google Fonts release, added 12 November 2026.
- **Inter** (`inter-variable.woff2`, weights 100–900): body text, forms and controls. Also used by the Filament teacher panel.

Manrope was the previous heading font. It was removed in the 12 November 2026 redesign; its source TTF stays in `assets/design/fonts/` for reference.
