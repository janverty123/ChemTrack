# Exploratory research-use pricing

28 October 2026, Asia/Manila. Estimates for discussion only; no commercial model or price approved. ChemTrack is still unbuilt.

| Offer | Suggested PHP range | Assumptions |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Research access | 5,000–10,000 per study | Completed existing product, limited cohort, 3 months, researcher supplies/manages content |
| Configured research package | 10,000–25,000 per study | Existing product plus bounded setup, branding/report changes, orientation and support |
| Dedicated custom build | 80,000–150,000+ | Separately scoped development; complex requirements can exceed this; ownership and operations priced explicitly |

These are proposed prices, not verified ChemTrack sales or established buyer demand. Access pricing assumes the core product is already funded and completed; it cannot fund an entire new custom build for the first client.

An offer to test could be PHP 10,000–15,000 per study: one teacher, up to 50 students, 3 months, existing learning/assessment features, bounded content setup, standard results export, orientation and defined basic support. This is illustrative scope, not tested capacity or existing functionality. Standard hosting could be included after costing; domains, extensions, major changes and extra support should be priced separately.

Researcher supplies content and approved questions. Instrument writing/validation, thesis writing, statistical interpretation, exclusive ownership and source-code transfer are outside an access package. Keep research-data rights and developer code rights explicit, with agreed study-end export and retention arrangements. Validate operating costs and willingness to pay before publishing prices.

## Public references checked

- [MoodleCloud](https://www.moodlecloud.com/standard-plans/): opened page showed AUD 230/year for 50 users, annual billing and potential country taxes. Hosted LMS comparison only; not equivalent research customization/support. No PHP conversion assumed.
- [Oliver Revelo](https://www.oliverrevelo.com/blog/cost-of-custom-web-app-development-philippines-2026): published simple custom MVP range of PHP 80,000–150,000, with larger platforms higher. One developer's guide for Next.js/React work, not a representative market survey or a ChemTrack quote.

No SaaS, billing, or multiple-customer architecture approved. If research access is selected, discuss study isolation, onboarding/support, operating costs and study-end exports before expanding the design. Learning-cycle clarification remains pending.
